About the objective

Pair repulsion 1/d + 1/(π − d), where d is the geodesic distance on S³. Symmetry images and antipodes are included. Barzilai–Borwein steps with curvature scaling.

Near field sums only the orbit points within 20 mean orientation radii, fading smoothly over the outer half, so a step costs time linear in the count. Its energy is the near-field sum. Differences in density at larger scales even out more slowly than with all pairs.

Moves disjoint groups of points around the largest empty caps and keeps a move only if the exact local maximum drops. Small kicks leave plateaus, and the best sample found is kept. Separation is not constrained and usually falls.
